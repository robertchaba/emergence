THE IDEA

Conditions are given. Life finds its way.

01 / WORLD

Geography sets the stage.

The world supplies terrain and physical conditions. Resources and changing seasons shape the possibilities for life.

02 / INHERITANCE

Every lineage has a beginning.

Descendants inherit from those before them. Mutation introduces variation; local conditions shape what persists.

03 / EMERGENCE

The outcome stays open.

No predefined species. No scripted outcomes. Ecology emerges from life, one generation at a time.
